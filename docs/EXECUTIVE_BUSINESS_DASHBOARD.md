# Executive Business Dashboard

Business-oriented KPI dashboard with synthetic financial metrics.

**⚠️ All financial values are synthetic demo data for illustrative purposes only.**

## Metrics

### Customer Impact
- Total affected customers
- Critical incident count
- Repeat incident rate

### Revenue Impact (Synthetic)
- Total estimated impact
- Per-incident average
- Clearly labeled as synthetic

### SLA Penalties (Synthetic)
- Potential exposure
- Breach count tracking

### Network Investment (Synthetic)
- Infrastructure spend
- Operations budget
- Maintenance allocation

### Operational Costs (Synthetic)
- Labor, maintenance, tools
- Cost per incident

### Risk Overview
- High-risk regions
- Critical assets at risk
- Compliance risks

`GET /api/business/dashboard` - Complete business dashboard data
