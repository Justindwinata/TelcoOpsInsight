# Product Requirements

## Product

TelcoOps Insight is a Network Operations and Service Assurance Dashboard for the fictional company **NusaTel Digital Network** and its Network Operations Center business unit.

## Users

- Network operations managers
- Service assurance analysts
- Field operations coordinators
- Portfolio reviewers evaluating a full-stack telecom analytics prototype

## Goals

- Show a professional internal operations dashboard, not a simple chart demo.
- Provide realistic synthetic data across sites, incidents, SLA, tickets, technicians, regional performance, and service quality.
- Persist sample data locally in SQLite.
- Expose deterministic analytics through FastAPI.
- Present KPIs, charts, tables, upload validation, recommendations, and reports in React.

## Non-Goals

- No real Telkom data, branding, trademark, or official identity.
- No live NOC integration.
- No real-time streaming.
- No OSS/BSS, CRM, or ERP integration.
- No authentication, enterprise SSO, or production security controls in TOI-0001.
- No AI or machine-learning prediction.

## Scenario

NusaTel Digital Network operates Fiber Internet, Mobile Broadband, Enterprise VPN, IPTV, Voice, and Cloud Connectivity services across Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, Medan, Makassar, Semarang, Yogyakarta, Denpasar, Palembang, and Balikpapan.

Operational teams include NOC Core, Field Operations, Customer Assurance, Fiber Maintenance, and Enterprise Support.
